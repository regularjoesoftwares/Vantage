Quick answers

I completed a task by mistake — how do I get it back?

At the bottom of your task list, under Completed today, press and hold the task to restore it. For something finished on an earlier day, open Review → Looking back and press and hold it there.

Where is my data, and is it backed up?

Everything stays on your device — Vantage has no account and no servers. It also keeps automatic backups: to your iCloud Drive when that's enabled, otherwise on the device itself. Settings has a Back up now button and a Restore option if you ever need them.

How do the trial and the price work?

Vantage is free for 14 days. You can extend the trial twice by sending a bit of feedback along the way. After that, a single purchase unlocks it for good — there is no subscription.

Voice capture isn't working.

Vantage needs Microphone and Speech Recognition permission — check Settings → Vantage on your device. If a word gets misheard, the confirmation screen lets you fix any field before the task is saved.

How do I add a task with Siri?

Say "Hey Siri, add a task in Vantage." Siri asks what the task is, then whether you'd like to add another, and loops until you're done.
