Vantage is built around three simple ideas.

1

Capture without friction

Get something out of your head immediately; Vantage organizes the details.

Say what's on your mind inside Vantage, or use Siri without even opening the app. Vantage pulls out the important details so you don't have to fill in a form.

2

Know what to do next

Quickly turn your list into a realistic day instead of spending your time trying to prioritize.

Choose what fits today, see how much time you can commit, and spend less time deciding what to do next.

3

Move your life forward

Review progress, reconnect tasks to goals, and decide where you want your life to go.

Vantage brings your completed work, goals, milestones, and upcoming plans together so you can see what deserves attention next — and live more intentionally.

From thought to task, without the paperwork.

Capturing something shouldn't become another task.

Just say it

Speak naturally instead of working through a series of fields.

Vantage understands what you mean

Then get on with your day

The task lands where it belongs, ready when you need it.

A task list should answer a question.

What should I do now?

Vantage helps you turn a long list into a realistic plan for today. See how much time you can commit, choose what fits, and spend less time repeatedly deciding what deserves your attention.

The goal isn't to build the perfect task list. It's to spend less time looking at the list.

Focus on one thing.

Once you've decided what matters, Vantage gets the rest of the list out of the way.

Work on one task, keep track of the time, and move to the next when you're ready.

Look back. Then look forward.

Review what you accomplished, see how your goals are moving, notice what needs attention, and decide where you want to go next.

Planning isn't only about catching up. It's also about making sure the things you're doing are taking you somewhere you want to go — and helping you live more intentionally.

Your tasks are supposed to serve your life.

Productivity can easily become an end in itself. Vantage is designed to help you get things done while keeping sight of why you're doing them in the first place — and to help you make choices about how you want to spend your time and where you want your life to go.

Do what matters. Then go live your life.
